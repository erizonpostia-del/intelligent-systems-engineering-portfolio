# Team physical TurtleBot deployment

## Team setting

Our team used a TurtleBot3 Burger with Ubuntu 22.04, ROS2 Humble, and Cartographer in an environment hosted on a teammate's computer. This setting is separate from my personal ROS1 simulation environment.

## My contribution

I contributed to TurtleBot assembly, hardware preparation, network configuration and troubleshooting, robot-to-PC communication, mapping-data acquisition and processing, and key mapping and goal-point movement operations.

## Result boundary

The available evidence supports a team-based physical goal-point movement demonstration. It includes manual keyboard teleoperation and RViz goal-directed movement context. I do not claim that I independently configured the entire workstation, independently completed the full physical workflow, or demonstrated continuous autonomous target-selection-to-arrival navigation.

## Public evidence

The public image is a redacted Navigation2 context image. The original video, team workspace, and raw records remain private.
