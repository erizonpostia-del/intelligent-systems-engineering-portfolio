# My contributions

## Personal ROS1 simulation

I implemented `house_auto_avoid.py` and modified its launch file for sector-based LiDAR obstacle handling in Gazebo. I also implemented `world_outer_loop.py` with the ROS `move_base` action interface. The available source, log, and terminal evidence support a nine-goal navigation sequence in simulation.

## Team physical TurtleBot deployment

The physical TurtleBot3 Burger deployment was completed as a team in a ROS2 environment hosted on a teammate's computer. My contribution focused on TurtleBot assembly, hardware preparation, network configuration and troubleshooting, robot-to-PC communication, mapping-data acquisition and processing, and key mapping and goal-point movement operations. I do not claim sole ownership of the workstation or sole completion of the physical deployment.

## Learning-based control

I independently completed the DQN implementation and experimental workflow for the course project. The instructor-confirmed boundary excludes the third-party Flappy Bird Gymnasium environment and standard software dependencies. I later extended the work with observation, training-budget, and algorithm-structure studies, including seed-controlled evaluation and CSV-based comparison.
