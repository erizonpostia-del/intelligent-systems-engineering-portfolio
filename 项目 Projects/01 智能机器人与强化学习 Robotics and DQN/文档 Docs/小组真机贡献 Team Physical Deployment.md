# Team-Based Physical TurtleBot Deployment / 小组真机贡献

## Environment Ownership

The physical TurtleBot ROS and terminal environment was hosted on a teammate's computer. It was separate from the user's personal ROS1 Ubuntu virtual machine.

The physical robot was a TurtleBot3 Burger. The team environment used Ubuntu 22.04, ROS2 Humble, and Cartographer (user-confirmed). Its ROS and terminal environment remained hosted on a teammate's computer.

## Team Context

Physical mapping and navigation were completed as a team experiment. The available material is therefore **team-based physical robot evidence hosted on a teammate's computer**.

## User Contributions

- TurtleBot assembly and hardware preparation.
- Network configuration, connection testing, and troubleshooting.
- Robot-to-PC communication setup.
- Mapping-data acquisition and related processing.
- Key mapping operations and key navigation operations during the team experiment.
