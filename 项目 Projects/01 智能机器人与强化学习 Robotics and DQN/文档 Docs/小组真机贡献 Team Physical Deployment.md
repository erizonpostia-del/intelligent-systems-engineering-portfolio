The physical TurtleBot ROS and terminal environment was hosted on a teammate's computer. It was separate from the user's personal ROS1 Ubuntu virtual machine.

Physical mapping and navigation were completed as a team experiment. The available material is therefore **team-based physical robot evidence hosted on a teammate's computer**.

- Robot-to-PC communication setup.
- Mapping-data acquisition and related processing.
