# NUS — MSc Artificial Intelligence & Innovation

## Priority portfolio items

1. [04 — Fire & Smoke Detection](../../projects/04-fire-smoke-detection/) — shows applied AI development through data governance, class-level evaluation, difficult-target review, and explicit next-step decisions.
2. [02 — Machine Learning Evaluation](../../projects/02-ml-evaluation/) — shows disciplined model selection with train-only preprocessing, held-out testing, and nested CV.
3. [01 — Robotics & DQN](../../projects/01-robotics-dqn/) — shows system integration across ROS, sensing, navigation, and learning-based control.

These links are selected for evidence of engineering judgement and cross-layer integration. They are not a claim about programme admissions criteria beyond the programme name supplied for this guide.

## Evidence boundary

Project 04 is validation/development evidence and does not claim deployment readiness. Project 02 uses synthetic `make_regression` data. Project 01 separates personal simulation, team physical work, and DQN evidence.
