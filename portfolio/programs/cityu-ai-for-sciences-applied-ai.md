## Fit and further learning

The published CityUHK **Track 4: Applied AI** lists subjects covering **smart-city AI, embodied AI, medical image analysis, and other applied/interdisciplinary topics**. My industrial-vision and robotics coursework supply initial system-facing experience; the medical-review and regression projects supply analysis and evaluation discipline.

I want to deepen embodied AI, generative-AI methods, study design, and deployment under real data and operating constraints. The present portfolio does **not** demonstrate a general-purpose embodied agent, clinical deployment, a medical segmentation training run, or independent production acceptance.
