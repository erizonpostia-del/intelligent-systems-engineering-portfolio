# NUS — MSc Precision Health & Medicine × AI

## Priority portfolio items

1. [03 — Medical Segmentation Review](../../projects/03-medical-segmentation-review/) — provides domain-aware discussion of medical segmentation, foundation-model adaptation, prompting, and evaluation.
2. [02 — Machine Learning Evaluation](../../projects/02-ml-evaluation/) — demonstrates transparent preprocessing, model selection, and nested-CV methodology.
3. [04 — Fire & Smoke Detection](../../projects/04-fire-smoke-detection/) — adds applied perception evidence with class-level analysis and data-governance reasoning.

These links foreground scientific reading, evaluation discipline, and awareness of domain-specific evidence requirements.

## Evidence boundary

Project 03 is a literature-based technical review; it contains no patient-data experiment, model training, fine-tuning, or project-generated medical metrics. Project 02 is a simulated regression study. Project 04 is not medical research and does not claim deployment readiness.
