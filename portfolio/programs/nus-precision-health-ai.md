## Programme alignment and learning goals

The NUS Medicine programme includes precision genomics, proteomics/metabolomics, statistical analysis, high-performance computing, and machine learning for precision medicine. My preparation is strongest in **reading AI-method literature and evaluating models**; it is weaker in **molecular biology, genomics, clinical interpretation, and high-dimensional biomedical datasets**. Those are explicit postgraduate learning goals.

Project 03 is **not** an executed medical-image segmentation experiment. It includes no patient dataset, project-trained/fine-tuned weights, or self-computed medical Dice/IoU scores. Similarly, Project 04 is forest-monitoring work, **not** medical AI validation.
