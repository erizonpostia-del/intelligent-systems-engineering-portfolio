## Fit and intended development

The CUHK programme includes **machine learning and AI foundations, mathematics/statistics, AI ethics, and applications to scientific domains**. Project 03 demonstrates disciplined technical synthesis; Project 02 demonstrates quantitative evaluation; Project 04 shows how data assumptions affect applied inference. I seek more depth in mathematical modelling, applied deep learning, and experimental collaboration with domain scientists.

This is **not** a record of original biomedical discovery, a medical-image model training run, or a peer-reviewed paper. Project 03 is a literature-based review; Project 02 uses `make_regression`; Project 04 records validation-stage work outside healthcare.
