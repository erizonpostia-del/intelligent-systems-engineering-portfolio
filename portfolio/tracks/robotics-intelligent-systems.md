# Robotics & Intelligent Systems

**Focus:** navigation, sensing, robot-system integration, learning-based decision experiments, and the boundary between simulation and physical validation.

## Two-minute evidence path

1. **[01 — Robotics & DQN](../../projects/01-robotics-dqn/OVERVIEW.md)**: personal ROS1/Gazebo LiDAR and nine-goal waypoint sequence; separately attributed team ROS2/TurtleBot3 physical work; independent FlappyBird-v0 DQN architecture comparisons.
2. **[04 — Fire & Smoke Detection](../../projects/04-fire-smoke-detection/OVERVIEW.md)**: computer-vision perception workflow, checkpoint auditing, per-class diagnosis, and governance of development data.
3. **[02 — Machine Learning Evaluation](../../projects/02-ml-evaluation/OVERVIEW.md)**: reproducible model-selection methods, train-only feature preprocessing, and nested CV on synthetic data.

The key technical narrative is **robot interface integration → perception and data constraints → credible experimental evaluation**, not an invented combined robot-learning product.

## Contribution and evidence boundary

ROS1 nine-goal navigation is **simulation-only**. TurtleBot3 physical demonstration is **team-owned**, with individual contributions documented. DQN was tested in **FlappyBird-v0**, not deployed on a robot. No novel planner, complete physical autonomous mission, or production perception system is claimed.
