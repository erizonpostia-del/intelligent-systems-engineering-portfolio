# AI for Science / Health

**Focus:** scientific interpretation, medical-domain AI literature, trustworthy experimental design, and limitations of transferring models across datasets.

## Two-minute evidence path

1. **[03 — Medical Segmentation Review](../../projects/03-medical-segmentation-review/OVERVIEW.md)**: SAM, SAM-Path, MedSAM, adaptation mechanisms, metrics, and evidence traceability. **Literature review only**.
2. **[02 — Machine Learning Evaluation](../../projects/02-ml-evaluation/OVERVIEW.md)**: a runnable synthetic-data demonstration of train-only scaling, validation-based selection, held-out testing, and nested CV.
3. **[04 — Fire & Smoke Detection](../../projects/04-fire-smoke-detection/OVERVIEW.md)**: an **industrial**, not medical, example of data governance and per-class error analysis.

The route moves from **domain literature → methodology → transferable engineering evaluation**. It does not turn nonmedical work into clinical evidence.

## Claims not made

No original medical dataset, fine-tuning, patient-data study, clinical validation, or independent medical segmentation benchmark is published. The regression datasets are synthetic. The forest-monitoring detector is not a healthcare application.
